How the price is worked out

Labor is hours times cleaners times the pay you want. Supplies and driving are added on top, then overhead covers the costs that don't belong to one job, like insurance and your phone bill. The margin is money for the business itself, and the total is rounded up to the nearest $5.

Never quote below the break-even price. If clients push back, cut the scope (skip the oven, do every other week) rather than your rate.
