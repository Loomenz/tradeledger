Tax deductions house cleaners often miss

Every business expense you track lowers the profit you're taxed on. These are the ones cleaners most often forget. Always check with a tax professional for your situation.

  • Mileage between clients and to buy supplies. Try the mileage calculator.
  • Supplies: chemicals, cloths, gloves, trash bags.
  • Equipment: vacuums, mops, steamers, caddies.
  • Phone and software: the business share of your phone bill and any booking or invoicing apps.
  • Insurance and bonding.
  • Advertising: flyers, ads, business cards, website.
  • Uniforms with your logo.
  • Training and licenses.
  • Payments to helpers or subcontractors.

The catch: you need records

Deductions only help if you can show them. Keep receipts and a running log through the year, not in a panic in April.
