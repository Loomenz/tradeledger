How much to charge for Airbnb turnover cleaning

Turnovers are fast, frequent and time-boxed. Price them as a flat rate per unit, then add the extras that hosts forget to mention.

Start with a flat rate by size

UnitTypical timeHow to set your price
Studio or 1 bed1 to 1.5 hoursYour target hourly pay times hours, plus supplies and driving
2 bed1.5 to 2.5 hoursSame formula, and time your first clean
3+ bed2.5 to 4 hoursQuote after a walkthrough, never sight unseen

Rates vary a lot by city, so work from your own numbers rather than someone else's price list. The price calculator turns your hours and costs into a flat rate.

Charge separately for the extras

  • Laundry: per load, or per set of linens if you take them off site.
  • Restocking: a small fee plus the cost of the supplies, or have the host supply them.
  • Same-day or tight windows: a rush fee when checkout and check-in are only a few hours apart.
  • Damage or deep-clean surprises: agree an hourly rate in writing before the first job.
  • Photos and checklists: hosts value proof. Build it into your price instead of doing it free.

Protect your margin

Turnovers live or die on driving time. Group units that are close together, add a minimum charge, and track which hosts actually make you money. A profit tracker shows your real hourly rate per client, so you know who to raise and who to drop.
