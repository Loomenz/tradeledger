Simple bookkeeping for a cleaning business

You don't need accounting software to start. You need four lists and ten minutes a week.

The four lists

  1. Jobs: date, client, price, tip, and whether they've paid.
  2. Expenses: date, what it was, where, and how much.
  3. Mileage: date, where you drove, and how many miles.
  4. Clients: who they are and what you usually charge them.

Your 10-minute weekly routine

  1. Add this week's jobs and mark who has paid.
  2. Snap receipts and add them to expenses.
  3. Add your miles.
  4. Chase anyone still unpaid.
  5. Move your tax set-aside into a separate savings account.

Numbers worth watching

  • Profit, not revenue.
  • Real hourly rate, after costs.
  • Unpaid invoices.
  • Top clients, so you know who to protect.

You can keep these in any spreadsheet, or use a ready-made one that adds everything up for you.
